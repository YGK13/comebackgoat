## What it is to the user (L2)
- Buyer and user: Laid-off or exposed senior executive who could earn from people who already know their work.
- Where it fits in their day: Week three after the layoff, after the 40th application. We get the moment of "this is not working".
- Live user sentence today: H1 "Stop applying. Start getting paid." CTA "Apply" and "Apply for a seat". Line "Your business development team, in Claude."
- Candidate user sentences (DRAFT):
  1. Stop applying. Start getting paid. (keep)
  2. Your fastest money is people who already trust you.
  3. AI took the job. Your old network still pays.
  4. GOAT, come back.
  5. Your first client is someone who already knows your work.
- Competitor-swap test: Ours.

## Category and rank (L3)
- Category word we use: Coined candidates: "revenue insurance" (Yuri's term) and "the boomerang" (DRAFT).
- Number one for what: Executives turning people who already trust them into paid work in 14 days.
- Coined category: "GOAT, come back."
- Safe-choice line: Not B2B.

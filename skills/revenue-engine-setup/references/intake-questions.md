# Intake questions

Ask only what the harvest did not answer with a source. Group into at most 3 rounds of up to 4 questions. Offer choices; the user can always type their own answer.

## Core (always, unless answered with a source)

1. **Runway and target.** "Roughly how many months can you go without new income, and what monthly income do you need to feel safe?"
   Choices: under 3 months / 3 to 6 / 6 to 12 / 12+. Then a free-text monthly target.
2. **Money appetite** (multi-select). Client projects and retainers / No-equity grants, prizes and fellowships / A job as insurance / Equity investment / Loans or credit lines.
3. **Hard constraints** (free text, prompt with examples): "Where can you live and work? How often can you travel? Any days or hours you never work? Languages? Citizenship or work-authorization facts that applications ask about?"
4. **Job insurance stance.** Not interested / Only if a great fit appears / Actively want a job now. Then: floor compensation, full-time vs contract, and "Must this stay invisible to your clients?" (stealth mode).
5. **Never-say list.** "Any names, employers, numbers, affiliations or topics that must never appear in anything I draft?"

## Lane gaps (only for lanes the user turned on)

**Grants and free money**
- Entity: legal name, type (sole prop, LLC, S-corp, C-corp, nonprofit), state, formation date, active status. EIN: only "yes / not yet". Never the number.
- Revenue stage: pre-revenue / under $50K / $50K to $250K / $250K+ (last 12 months).
- Optional and explained: "Some grants are only for businesses owned by women, veterans, people of color, people with disabilities or in rural or low-income areas. Want me to screen for any of these? Skip is fine." Store only what they choose to share.
- Will they relocate or attend in person for a program? For how long?

**Projects and clients**
- Rate card: day rate, project minimum, retainer range. If unknown, offer to propose ranges later from market data, labelled as estimates.
- Engagement types they want: advisory, fractional role, project, workshop or training, speaking, expert calls, subcontracting to firms.
- Industries and company sizes they serve best; any they refuse.

**Job insurance**
- Target titles (2 to 5), adjacent titles, seniority, remote rules, time zones, floor compensation and currency, deal-breakers.
- Daily cap on prepared applications (default 5; maximum 10).

**Money sweep**
- Every US state (and country) lived or worked in, with rough years; former names; past employers with retirement plans; businesses closed or dissolved.

## Style of asking

- One sentence of why for any sensitive question.
- Accept "I don't know" and record it as an open question in `profile.md`, never as a guess.
- After the last round, read back the five core answers in one short list and ask "Right?" once.

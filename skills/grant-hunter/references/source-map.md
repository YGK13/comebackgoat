# Source map

Categories, not a list of open programs: programs open and close constantly, so every item is verified live on the funder's own page at run time. Examples are starting points to search, not endorsements. Add productive sources to `trackers/learnings.md`; drop dead ones.

| Category | Fits | Where to look (examples) | Typical size |
|---|---|---|---|
| Small business grants (corporate and nonprofit sponsored) | for-profit sole props and LLCs, often by stage, sector or owner demographics | Hello Alice grant listings, NASE growth grants, corporate programs announced by banks, telecoms and shipping companies, women-owned business grant programs | $500 to $25K, high competition |
| State, city and economic development programs | businesses located in that state or city | state economic development agency, city small business services, Small Business Development Centers (SBDC), chambers of commerce | varies; often reimbursement or matched |
| Federal R&D (SBIR and STTR) | for-profit US small businesses with a genuine R&D project | sbir.gov solicitations by agency | larger, long cycles, real technical bar |
| Federal assistance listings | specific eligible applicants | grants.gov, SAM.gov assistance listings | rarely for solo consultants; read eligibility first |
| Founder fellowships and no-equity grants | individual builders and founders | Emergent Ventures (Mercatus Center), founder grant newsletters, AI builder grant programs | $1K to $100K |
| Prizes, challenges, hackathons | anyone meeting the challenge rules | challenge.gov, Devpost, foundation prize programs, sector challenges | $1K to $1M+, outcome-based |
| Pitch competitions | early-stage companies | universities and alumni networks, accelerators, regional startup weeks, women and minority founder events | $1K to $50K; travel may be required |
| Cloud, AI and software credits | founders building software | Anthropic, AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft for Startups, Vercel, Supabase, Stripe partner perks | credits, not cash; still cuts burn |
| Foundation grants (nonprofit lane) | registered nonprofits or fiscally sponsored projects | Kindora connector (search_open_grants, search_funders, find_funders_for_peers), Candid, community foundations | $5K to $500K+ |
| Research and thought-leadership grants | experts producing research or public-interest work | foundations funding work on the user's topic, university centers, think-tank fellowships | $5K to $100K |
| Sponsorships | creators with an audience (newsletter, podcast, events) | brands in the audience's buying path, sponsorship marketplaces | per issue or per event |
| Opportunity newsletters and aggregators | all | the user's subscribed newsletters (from setup), fundsforngos, Opportunity Desk, F6S, Devpost | leads only; verify at the source |

## Search patterns

- `"<focus term>" grant OR fellowship OR prize "applications open" <current month> <current year>`
- `site:linkedin.com/posts ("applications open" OR "call for proposals") <focus term>`
- `<state> small business grant <current year> site:.gov`
- `<sector> pitch competition <current year> virtual`
Always add the current year; stale programs dominate results otherwise.

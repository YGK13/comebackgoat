# Scoring rubric (0 to 100)

Weights come from `lanes.yaml` (defaults below). Score each factor 0 to 1, multiply by its weight, round.

| Factor | Default weight | 1.0 means | 0.0 means |
|---|---|---|---|
| fit | 30 | the program's stated priority is the user's exact focus and every eligibility row passed | generic program, eligible on a technicality |
| value | 25 | cash at or above the user's monthly target, no equity, or a door-opener (multi-year funder, accelerator network) | credits of little use, tiny amount |
| odds | 20 | small, defined applicant pool or a warm path (an alumnus, a past winner in the user's network) | open lottery with thousands of applicants |
| effort | 15 | a short form the package already answers | a full proposal, budget, letters and a video |
| urgency | 10 | deadline in 4 to 14 days | deadline 90+ days away (can wait) |

"Requested by the user" items skip scoring and go to the top.

# Scam and pay-to-play filter

Drop the item and note why if any of these is true. The first five follow the FTC's consumer alert on government grant scams (March 5, 2026).

1. Unsolicited contact saying the user "qualifies" for free money.
2. Grant money pitched for personal expenses (bills, debt, home repairs) rather than a defined program.
3. A request for a Social Security number, ID number or bank account number to "check eligibility" or "deposit" the grant.
4. Any fee to receive, process or "release" a grant, especially by gift card, wire, crypto or cash.
5. Pressure to act today, or a claimed government agency that the official .gov directory does not list.
6. "Awards" or "top 40 under 40" lists that charge to be named, to accept or for a mandatory gala ticket or plaque.
7. Paid "grant finder" or "guaranteed grant" services.
8. A program page with no named organization, no physical address or no past winners that can be verified independently.
9. Prize terms that transfer the user's IP or take equity without saying so in the headline.

Borderline (keep but flag in Notes): modest application fees for legitimate competitions, travel at the applicant's cost, programs funded by a vendor that requires using its product.

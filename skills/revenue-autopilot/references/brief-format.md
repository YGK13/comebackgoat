# Revenue Brief format

```
Revenue Brief, <weekday> <date>

CLAUDE DID (<n>): 2 follow-ups drafted, 1 form filled to Submit, 3 leads researched

NEEDS YOU (<n>, about <m> min)
1. R1 M-3 Unpaid invoice, Client B ($4,200, 45 days): send reminder draft. 3 min
2. R3 P-12 Acme Health - fractional CHRO inquiry: send draft (reply in thread). 3 min
3. R5 G-7 State SBDC microgrant ($5,000, due Nov 14): submit (tab open). 3 min

DEADLINES (7 days)
Nov 9  J-21 Northwind - AI Enablement Lead: form filled, Submit waits
Nov 14 G-7 State SBDC microgrant

SINCE YESTERDAY
- Reply from Client C: wants a call next week (P-4)
- Confirmed: $1,180 platform payout released (M-2)

PIPELINE  money found $5,380 confirmed | projects 9 waiting | grants 3 waiting | jobs 5 ready
Reply with what to send or submit, for example: send P-12, submit G-7.
```

Order "Needs you" exactly as the day's plan does: deadlines inside 48 hours, then R1 to R3, then the rest, by expected value. Never show more than 7 items; say "+ n more in the trackers".

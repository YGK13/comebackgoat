# Boomerang offers and drafts

## Consultant first (default)

A 60 to 90 day scoped engagement on the problem the signal shows:
- **Problem** in the company's own words (from the signal source).
- **Approach** in 3 steps the user has done before (from `claims.yaml` proof stories, ideally at this company if the user may cite it).
- **Shape:** advisory retainer, fixed-scope project or interim leadership, from `lanes.yaml` engagement types.
- **Fee** as a range from the rate card. Never below the project minimum.
- **Exit ramp:** "If it works, we can talk about something longer." This is the bridge to a rehire on the user's terms.

## Rehire (only with `rehire_ok: true`)

Route to job-insurance with the insider as the referral path. Suggest the user ask the insider for a referral before applying; referred candidates skip the cold queue.

## Draft rules

- To the insider, not to HR, unless HR is the warmest contact.
- Under 120 words. Open with the shared history in one line, then the signal, then one ask: a 20-minute catch-up with the booking link.
- No pitch deck, no rate in the first note, no criticism of the company or of anyone who stayed.
- In the user's voice (`voice.md`), facts only from `claims.yaml`.

Example shape (rewrite in the user's voice every time):

> Hi Sam - it's been a while since the Q3 offsite. Saw the news that Acme is rolling out AI agents across the service team; I spent much of last year doing exactly that at two other companies. Would you be open to a 20-minute catch-up? Happy to share what worked and what didn't. Here is my calendar if easier: <booking link>.

## The campaign line

The brand line "GOAT, come back." is the company speaking. The product's answer is "On my terms": come back as the consultant first.

# Source checklist

Mark every row `found`, `nothing` or `could not check (why)`. The ledger's "Could not check" list comes from this table.

| # | Source | How | Typical speed to cash |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Sent invoices and SOWs with no payment confirmation | email queries R1-R3 | days |
| A2 | Signed work never invoiced | email query R3, calendar of delivered sessions | days |
| A3 | Accounting and payments tools (QuickBooks AR aging, Stripe and PayPal balances and pending payouts, Mercury or bank receivables) | connectors, read-only | days |
| B1 | Platform payouts and wallets | email queries P1-P3 | days to weeks |
| B2 | Royalties and creator platforms (KDP, newsletters, YouTube, courses, affiliates) | email query P2 | weeks |
| C1 | Refunds, credits, deposits, reimbursements | email queries F1-F3 | days to weeks |
| C2 | Travel and airline credits with expiry | email query F3 | use before expiry |
| D1 | Corporate exit audit (left a job in the last 24 months) | corporate-exit-audit.md | weeks; deadlines matter most |
| E1 | Dormancy and escheatment notices | email query D1 | weeks |
| E2 | Retirement plans from every past employer | email query D2, files, DOL lost-and-found | weeks to months |
| E3 | HSA and FSA balances | email query D3 | FSA has a hard run-out date |
| F1 | Tax forms received (every payer is a lead) | files and email query T1 | varies |
| F2 | Tax refunds or amended-return opportunities | ask a CPA; list facts only | months |
| G1 | State unclaimed property, every state lived or worked in, personal and business names | public-registries.md | weeks |
| G2 | Savings bonds | public-registries.md | weeks |
| G3 | Class-action settlements with open claim windows | email query S1, public-registries.md | months |
| H1 | Country modules (lived or worked abroad) | public-registries.md | varies |
| L1 | Bank and card exports, 12+ months, every account | `spend-audit.md` stage 1 | savings start next cycle |
| L2 | Billing emails: receipts, renewals, price changes, trials, failed payments | `spend-query-bank.md` L1-L11 | next cycle |
| L3 | Fees: card FX, ATM, bank, late, interest | transactions plus query L9 | next cycle |
| L4 | Browser history: billing and plan pages visited | connected browser or history export | next cycle |
| L5 | Re-shop renewing bills (phone, internet, energy, insurance, mailbox, domains, software) | `reshop-and-scripts.md` | at renewal |
| L6 | Debt plans (student loans, card APRs) and pension or insurance fees | query L12-L13, country module | months; often the largest yearly saving |

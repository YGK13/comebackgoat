# Re-shop, then draft

## Re-shop method

For each renewing bill:
1. Current plan, price and renewal date from the receipt.
2. 2 to 3 current offers from the providers' own pages or a dated comparison site, each with link and "checked <date>". Include the price after any promo ends and setup fees.
3. Switching cost in plain words: number porting, address changes on other accounts, a domain move, lost history.
4. Verdict: stay and negotiate, downgrade, switch or cancel, with the yearly saving as "estimate" unless both prices are sourced.

## Draft templates (Gmail drafts only; the user sends)

Keep every draft under 120 words, one ask, the account named by email or last 4 digits only.

**Cancel**
Subject: Cancel <plan> for <account email>
Please cancel my <plan> effective at the end of the current period and confirm in writing that no further charges will be made. If any charge has posted since <date>, please refund it.

**Downgrade or retention**
Subject: Moving to a lower plan or cancelling - <plan>
I have been a customer since <date>. At <price> this no longer fits my budget, and <competitor> offers <comparable> for <price> (checked <date>). Can you offer a lower plan or a loyalty price? If not, please cancel at the end of the current period.

**Refund (charge after cancellation, duplicate, unused)**
Subject: Refund request - <charge date> <amount>
On <date> I was charged <amount> for <plan>. <Reason: I cancelled on <date> / this duplicates the charge on <date> / I have not used the account since <date>>. Please refund it to the original payment method and confirm.

**Fee waiver**
Subject: Fee review for my account ending <last 4>
I was charged <fee> on <date>. Please waive it and tell me which account plan has the lowest fees for my usage.

**Call script (providers that negotiate by phone)**
1. "I'm reviewing my bills and I'm about to switch. Before I do, what's the best price you can offer to keep me?"
2. Quote the competitor offer with its price and date.
3. Ask for the retention or loyalty department if the first answer is no.
4. Get the new price, the end date of any promo and the cancellation terms in writing (SMS or email).

## US module

| Lever | What to check | Who decides |
|---|---|---|
| Federal student loans | after rehabilitation or any servicer transfer, apply for an income-driven plan before the first bill; autopay often gives a rate cut | studentaid.gov; a student-loan counselor for the plan choice |
| Card FX fees | foreign-currency spend on a card charging 1 to 3%; use a no-FX-fee card or a local account | the user |
| Payment processor fees | negotiate only above about $50K a year of volume | the processor |
| Domains | renewals spread across registrars; consolidate at the registrar's renewal price, not the first-year price | the user |
| Virtual mailbox | seat count and plan versus cheaper providers; an address change touches every account that uses it | the user |
| SaaS | seats, overlap, monthly versus annual, features already included elsewhere | the user |

## Israel module (worked example; add one per country)

| Lever | What to check | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile | plans with 100GB+ cost far less than legacy plans; compare the 12-month average after promos and SIM fees | TheMarker Supermarker cellular comparison; providers' sites |
| Electricity | private suppliers discount the consumption component only, 5 to 7.5% around the clock or more in time windows | TheMarker Supermarker electricity comparison |
| Pension fees | compare the fund's deposit and accumulation fees with the default funds' tender rates; ask the fund for a reduction in writing | the fund; a licensed pension adviser |
| Insurance duplicates | the Ministry of Finance "Har HaBituach" lookup lists every policy; duplicates with the kupat holim supplementary plan are common | harb.cma.gov.il (user signs in) |
| Arnona | income-based and other municipal discounts; one-time annual payment discounts | the municipality's discount page |
| Bank fees | the regulated basic fee track and a fee review | the bank, in writing |
| Card used for shekels | a US card with an FX fee on shekel bills costs more than the bank's own conversion | the user |

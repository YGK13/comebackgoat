# Corporate exit audit

For anyone who left a W-2 job in the last 24 months. This is where a former executive most often leaves real money on the table, almost always by missing a date. Output facts, dates and questions; never advice. Recommend an employment lawyer for agreement terms and a CPA or plan administrator for tax and plan questions.

Sources: the separation agreement, offer letter, equity plan documents and grant notices, benefits emails (queries X1-X3), the last 3 paystubs.

| Item | What to find | Why it matters | Who to ask |
|---|---|---|---|
| Severance payment schedule | amount (if stated), dates, conditions (non-compete, cooperation, non-disparagement) | a missed installment is a receivable; a breach risk can claw it back | employment lawyer |
| Release signing window | days to sign or revoke, if not yet signed | money can depend on signing inside the window | employment lawyer |
| Final pay and PTO payout | was accrued PTO paid where the policy or state law requires it | an unpaid payout is money owed | state labor department page, HR |
| Unreimbursed expenses | expense reports submitted before exit and not paid | receivable | former manager or HR |
| Bonus and commission | pro-rated or earned-but-unpaid amounts under the plan | receivable | plan document, HR |
| Stock options | grant dates, vested count, type (ISO or NSO), post-termination exercise deadline | options that expire unexercised are lost; ISOs generally lose ISO tax treatment if exercised more than 3 months after leaving, even when the plan allows longer | plan administrator, CPA |
| RSUs and vesting cliffs | next vest date that was missed by days, acceleration terms | sometimes negotiable before signing a release | employment lawyer |
| 401(k) and pension | balance, vesting, rollover status, loan outstanding (an unpaid loan can become taxable) | lost or stranded retirement money | plan administrator |
| HSA and FSA | FSA run-out deadline for claims; HSA balance and custodian | FSA money is forfeited after the run-out date | benefits administrator |
| Health coverage | COBRA election deadline; marketplace coverage as an alternative | avoids a costly gap; not a cash item but protects runway | benefits administrator |
| Tuition, certification or relocation repayment clauses | amounts the user may owe back | a liability to plan around | employment lawyer |
| Unemployment insurance | whether the state allows benefits alongside severance or self-employment start-up activity | possible cash; rules vary by state | state labor department |

Write each found item as a ledger row with its deadline. Anything with a deadline inside 30 days goes to the top of "Act this week".

# Scoring rubric (0 to 100)

Weights from `lanes.yaml` project_hunter.weights (defaults below). Score each factor 0 to 1, multiply, round.

| Factor | Default weight | 1.0 means | 0.0 means |
|---|---|---|---|
| fit | 30 | the need is one of the user's listed offers, for their ideal client | adjacent at best |
| value | 25 | likely fee at or above the project minimum, or a retainer | unpaid or below minimum |
| warm_path | 20 | a past client, colleague or 1st-degree connection is involved | fully cold |
| speed_to_cash | 15 | could start and invoice within 30 days | procurement cycle of 3+ months |
| effort | 10 | a short reply wins the meeting | a full RFP response with forms and registrations |

Inbound requests from real people get +10 (cap 100). Items from the user's own forwarded leads skip scoring and go to the top.

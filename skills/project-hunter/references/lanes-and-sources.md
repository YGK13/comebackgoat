# Lanes and sources

Turn lanes on or off via `lanes.yaml` project_hunter.engagement_types. Sources are leads; the opportunity's own page or the poster's own post is the proof.

| Lane | Best sources | Proof of live | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Inbound | the user's email (all addresses that reach this inbox) | the thread itself | fastest cash; always first |
| Warm reactivation | sent mail history, `Connections.csv`, CRM if connected, past invoices | a trigger event found on a company or news page | the highest-converting lane for new consultants |
| Fractional and interim roles | fractional job boards and communities, company career pages with "fractional" or "interim" titles, VC portfolio job boards | the posting on the company's own page | treat like jobs for verification, like clients for pitching |
| Advisory and project work | public LinkedIn and X posts ("looking for", "recommendations for a consultant"), founder communities, Reddit and Stack Exchange threads in the niche | the post itself, under 14 days old | reply with value first, no pitch in the first line |
| RFPs | state and city procurement portals, school districts, nonprofits' RFP pages, SAM.gov contract opportunities (for registered entities), association RFP boards | the solicitation page with its deadline | check registration requirements (SAM, state vendor) before preparing |
| Expert calls | the user's expert networks (GLG, AlphaSights, Guidepoint, Third Bridge, Dialectica and others they belong to) via inbound email | the request email | fit: draft yes; non-fit: draft a decline naming better topics |
| Workshops and training | L&D platforms, corporate academies, universities' executive education, associations' event calendars | the call for facilitators or the program page | rate as a day rate |
| Speaking and podcasts | conference calls for speakers, podcast guest requests, association events | the CFP page with deadline | visibility lane; value is pipeline, score accordingly |
| Subcontracting | boutique consultancies and agencies hiring specialists, platforms such as Catalant, Business Talent Group, A.Team, Toptal, Pluris | the request or listing | platforms add a client-side fee; quote net rate |

## Search patterns (always last 7 days, add current year)

- `site:linkedin.com/posts ("looking for a fractional" OR "need a fractional" OR "recommend a consultant") <niche>`
- `site:x.com ("looking for" OR "need help with") <niche> consultant`
- `"request for proposals" <service> <state or sector> <current year>`
- `"call for speakers" <topic> <current year>`
- `"podcast guests" <topic> <current year>`

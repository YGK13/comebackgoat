# Outreach rules

Every draft is plain text, in the user's voice (`context/voice.md`), signed with the contact block from `claims.yaml`. No em dashes unless the voice file uses them.

## Inbound reply (60 to 120 words)
Thank them by naming the specific request, answer the question asked, propose one concrete next step (a 20-minute call with the booking link, or 2 time windows that respect `constraints.schedule`), nothing else.

## Warm reactivation (70 to 110 words)
1. A specific, true reason for writing now (their trigger: funding, new role, launch, a post they wrote).
2. One line on what the user does now, from the messaging kit one-liner.
3. One relevant proof story in a sentence.
4. A soft ask: "Worth a short call?" or "Who on your team owns this?"
Never: "just checking in", "hope you're well" as the opener, a list of services, a discount.

## Public post reply (comment, under 400 characters)
Answer the poster's need with one useful idea first; then one line of fit; then "Happy to share more by DM." No links in the first comment.

## Cold email to a published address (90 to 130 words)
Subject names their need, not the user's service. Same structure as reactivation, with the proof chosen for their sector.

## Proposal outline (one page)
Their problem in their words / outcome in 90 days / approach in 3 steps / timeline / fee range from the rate card with the project minimum / one proof story / next step.

## Expert-call decline (40 to 70 words)
Thank them, say it is outside the user's expertise, list 3 topics they are strong on, ask the network to update their profile tags.
